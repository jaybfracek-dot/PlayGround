QUICK START

Scratch anything

No controller? Drag the platters, use the keyboard, or open the virtual MIDI controller.

1 · Load music

Import your MP3s or use the four built-in demo tracks. Drag files anywhere on the window. Audio is decoded locally with the Web Audio API.

2 · Scratch the platters

Drag a platter with the mouse or finger, scroll over it, or press Q/E for Deck A and O/P for Deck B. The read-head moves forward and backward through the audio buffer.

3 · Connect MIDI

Plug in a class-compliant controller, open MIDI Setup, enable MIDI and click LEARN next to any control. Or run AUTO-MAP and follow the seven steps.

Capacitive vs non-touch

If a Touch sensor is mapped, the platter is treated as capacitive: scratch while touched, pitch-nudge while rotating untouched. With no touch mapping, rotation auto-grabs the platter and scratches.

Relative jog signals

Jog ticks are decoded as 7-bit two's complement by default. If direction feels inverted, choose SIGNED BIT or ABSOLUTE in Platter Engine. Ticks per revolution calibrates scratch distance.

Keyboard

A/L play deck A/B · S/; cue · QE / OP scratch · ←→ crossfader · Space play/pause deck A.
