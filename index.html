HOW TO CUT

01

SCRATCH THE PLATTER

Drag either on-screen platter with a mouse or finger. The touch layer freezes the transport and the angular velocity becomes playback rate — forward, backward and everything between.

TOUCH→Δ ANGLE→RATE→AUDIO
02

CAPACITIVE VS NON-TOUCH

Capacitive platters send a touch note/CC. Touch freezes audio, rotation scratches, release resumes if the deck was playing. Non-touch platters have no sensor: jog rotation is interpreted as bend or scratch depending on transport state and the Vinyl setting.

03

RELATIVE ENCODERS

Jog wheels usually send relative CC deltas. PHASE//TWO decodes 2's complement, signed-bit, sign-magnitude, absolute 7-bit and 14-bit protocols. Each delta is scaled by ticks-per-revolution and mapped to scratch rate.

04

LOAD YOUR MP3s

Click Import MP3 or drop files anywhere. Files are decoded locally with the Web Audio API; ID3 title, artist and BPM tags are read when present. Nothing leaves your machine.
